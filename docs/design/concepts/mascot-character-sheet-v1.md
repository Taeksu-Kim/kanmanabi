# Mascot character sheet — v1

Status: superseded by v2; not approved for production use.

## Intent

A friendly young adult Korean woman who appears as a lightweight learning guide
at welcome, answer-feedback, and session-complete moments. She should not compete
with the question while the learner is answering.

## Generated states

1. Welcome — relaxed smile and small wave.
2. Correct — restrained fist-pump and brighter smile.
3. Encourage — calm expression and open-hand explanation gesture.

## Generation prompt

Create one consistent young adult Korean woman mascot in three clearly separated
full-body poses on a single landscape character sheet: welcome, correct-answer
celebration, and gentle encouragement after a wrong answer. Use a polished flat 2D
editorial app-illustration style that is friendly to adults, with clean rounded
shapes and a restrained expressive face. Keep the exact same identity, hairstyle,
proportions, outfit, and palette in every pose.

Give her a contemporary Seoul sensibility and one wearable modernized-hanbok outfit:
a short jeogori-inspired wrap jacket with an asymmetric collar and restrained
goreum-like tie, high-waisted wide trousers, and modern sneakers. Use leaf green
`#2F8F68`, strong green `#207653`, mint `#E9F7EF`, cool near-white `#F7FAF8`, ink
`#19352A`, and only a tiny reward-yellow `#F4C95D` accent. Make the silhouette
readable at roughly 100–160 CSS pixels.

No text, labels, logos, environment, school uniform, idol stagewear, sexualization,
child proportions, fantasy or palace hanbok, wedding attire, historical costume,
Japanese kimono or Chinese hanfu elements, celebrity likeness, animal mascot,
Duolingo imitation, photorealism, 3D, glossy gradients, or accessory clutter.

## Review notes

- Strong: identity consistency, readable silhouette at small size, supportive
  gestures, palette fit, low asset complexity.
- Concern: the outfit currently reads more as a friendly modern-hanbok instructor
  than contemporary Korean street fashion with a subtle hanbok influence.
- If retained, use the character primarily after answer submission and at session
  completion. Do not place a full-body figure beside the active question.
