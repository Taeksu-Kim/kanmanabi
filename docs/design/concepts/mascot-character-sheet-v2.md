## Change from v1

Keep the same character, three feedback gestures, small-size readability, and green
palette. Move the clothing from an obvious modernized hanbok toward contemporary
Seoul fashion: roughly 70% modern daily wear and 30% hanbok-inspired construction.

## Edit prompt

Change only the clothing in the supplied v1 sheet. Preserve facial identity, skin
tone, low-bun hairstyle, loose face-framing strands, body proportions, illustration
style, poses, gestures, expressions, spacing, scale, background, grounding shadows,
and the center-pose accent marks.

Replace the obvious pale jeogori and large traditional tie with a muted leaf-green
cropped collarless wrap jacket: a clean asymmetric overlap inspired by jeogori
construction, one small flat side-fastening tab, softly structured blazer-like
sleeves, and a pale mint crew-neck knit layer. Modernize the deep green wide-leg
trousers with crisp front pleats and a subtle asymmetric wrap waistband; retain
simple white low-top sneakers. The outfit should feel plausible at a present-day
Seoul design studio, gallery, café, or university. Keep the same outfit across all
three poses and preserve the existing palette.

Avoid changing the face, hair, poses, or composition. No crossed white traditional
collar, large goreum bow, ceremonial or historical attire, instructor or school
uniform, kimono, hanfu, stagewear, new accessories, text, logos, or watermark.
