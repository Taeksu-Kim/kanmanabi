# Mascot character sheet — v3

Status: approved as the POC mascot direction. Production extraction is deferred
until its placement is validated in the mobile feedback UI.

## Change from v2

Keep the accepted contemporary outfit, poses, palette, composition, and hair. Make
only the face less generic while retaining a warm, adult, supportive expression.

## Edit prompt

Refine only the face across all three poses: use a slightly shorter soft-square
face, subtly fuller cheeks, smaller warm almond-shaped eyes with a very low crease,
mostly straight brows with a gentle outer lift, one small beauty mark below the
outer corner of her left eye, a slightly asymmetric smile with a subtle left cheek
dimple, a natural defined nose, restrained coral lips, and minimal makeup. Keep the
three expressions distinct: relaxed welcome, genuine correct-answer joy, and calm
encouragement.

Preserve the low bun, loose face-framing strands, hair and skin colors, body, poses,
hands, gestures, v2 clothing, palette, line weight, shading, background, shadows,
accent marks, spacing, scale, and landscape composition. Do not redesign anything
below the chin. Avoid celebrity or idol likeness, exaggerated ethnicity, childlike
features, huge anime eyes, heavy makeup, a narrow V-line, new accessories, text,
logos, or watermark.

## Review notes

- Improved: eyes and face shape feel less doll-like; the asymmetry and beauty mark
  provide a repeatable identity detail at medium sizes.
- At approximately 150 CSS pixels, the overall expression remains legible while
  the beauty mark becomes a subtle rather than dominant identifier.
- The character still belongs primarily in feedback and completion moments.
